# Changelog

## 1.1.0 (2026-11-11)

- New: water ripple fix (`KCDWater.asi`). Ripples on water move at the real frame rate instead of 30 to 40 steps a second. The mod is now called 30 FPS Grass & Water Fix.
- The installers work together with Native DLSS Support (which contains the grass fix: the grass plugin is then stored as `KCDGrassFix.asi.off`) and with the other plugin mods (shared `version.dll`).
- The grass plugin is unchanged (same file as 1.0.0).
